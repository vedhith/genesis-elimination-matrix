03 — Substrate: What Kind of Thing Is Fundamental?

This document owns axis C. It answers: granted a graspable ground (B1), what kind of thing is it? For B2 grounds, C is ? by 02-genesis.md §5.

1. Axis C

The recognized fundamental kinds are: experiential (there is something it is like to be it), concrete non-experiential (physical stuff, fields, spacetime), and abstract (mathematical or informational structure). The ground may be any one of these, a neutral base underlying them, or more than one at once.

Code Value Meaning Example positions
C1 Experiential The ground is mind-like. Matter is what mind looks like from outside, or is a pattern in mind. Idealism (Berkeley, Kastrup); cosmopsychism (Goff, Shani); Advaita Vedanta; Yogācāra Buddhism; the owner's "feeling is the very thing from the start" (Turn 7).
C2 Concrete non-experiential The ground is physical: particles, fields, spacetime, or whatever physics bottoms out in. Experience is derived or illusory. Physicalism; materialism; the owner's Clutter / AI pillar.
C3 Abstract The ground is mathematical or informational structure. Physical stuff is what structure looks like when instantiated, or structure is all there is. Tegmark's mathematical universe; Wheeler's "it from bit"; digital physics (Zuse, Fredkin); ontic structural realism (Ladyman); the owner's Universe Code pillar.
C4 Neutral The ground is neither mental nor physical but gives rise to both. Neutral monism (Russell, James, Mach); dual-aspect monism (Spinoza on one reading; Pauli–Jung); Russellian monism (which is sometimes filed as C1).
C5 Plural More than one fundamental kind, irreducible to each other. Substance dualism (Descartes); property dualism (Chalmers); Samkhya's Purusha and Prakriti; Cartesian interactionism.

Exhaustiveness: one kind, a neutral base, or several kinds. The three kinds listed are the ones any current position uses. If a genuinely new kind were proposed, it would be added as C1' etc. and the partition would remain exhaustive by construction, since "one / neutral / several" does not depend on how many kinds there are.

2. The substrate ladder: where "messy" and "clean" live

The owner's observation (Turn 15) that "going down is messy, going up is clean" belongs here. It is a claim about levels of description, not about which C value is true. Every C position has levels.

  level                 description                     compressibility
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  persons, cultures     folk psychology, history        very high (few variables)
  organisms             biology, neuroscience           high
  molecules             chemistry                       medium
  particles, fields     quantum field theory            low (many degrees of freedom)
  ?                     quantum gravity / pre-geometry  unknown

Higher levels are "cleaner" because irrelevant microscopic detail washes out. This is the content of effective field theory, the renormalization group (Wilson), and Anderson's "More Is Different" (1972). It holds under C1, C2, C3 and C4 alike. It does not favor any of them, and it does not invert Hoffman's interface theory, which is about why our perceptual level is the one it is (fitness), not about which level is "true."

What the owner added that is worth keeping: the direction of value. The owner claims the benefit is in moving up levels, not in reaching the bottom. That is an evaluative claim and belongs in 06-morality.md §7, where it is treated as a candidate normative principle.

3. Interaction with axis D (authorship)

C D1a (higher-layer author) reading D2 (unauthored) reading
C1 A mind simulating minds. Reality is mind, no author needed.
C2 A physical civilization running a physical simulation on physical hardware. The simulated layer may be C3 from inside while the base is C2. Matter just exists.
C3 Code written by someone. Mathematics just exists; all consistent structures are real (Tegmark level IV).
C4 Rare; a neutral base is usually posited to avoid authors. Russell, James.
C5 Rare. Descartes (with D1b: God guarantees the two substances interact).

Note the important case C2-base with C3-inside: from inside a simulation, the laws look like pure information (C3), but the ground is physical hardware (C2). The owner's phrase "our code maybe came from the core code of the sandbox itself" is this case. The C value of our layer and of the ground can differ under D1a, so the address should carry both when it matters: C3(layer)/C2(ground).

4. What each C value predicts, if anything

C Distinctive prediction, if any Status at To Nyn
C1 None at the level of physics. Some idealists predict that a complete physics will still leave experience unexplained (the hard problem is permanent). Untestable directly; see 04-mind.md.
C2 A complete physics explains everything, including experience, or explains it away. Physics is incomplete; experience is unexplained. Not eliminated, not confirmed.
C3 Physical constants and laws are derivable from structural or computational principles; spacetime is emergent from information. Active research (holography, amplituhedron, emergent spacetime). No decisive result. See 09-tests.md.
C4 Same observable predictions as C2 plus permanence of the hard problem. Same as C1.
C5 Mental causation should leave a physical trace: energy non-conservation or law violation at the mind–matter interface. No such trace found. Interactionist C5 is under pressure; epiphenomenal C5 is untestable.

5. Relationship to the owner's questions

  • "Are we the information the neuron holds?" (Turn 6): yes under C3; yes as a level-of-description claim under C2; the question is ill-posed under C1 (the neuron is in the information, not the reverse).
  • "Is the brain cell all we are?" (Turn 9): C2 says yes, C3 says the pattern is what matters and the cell is one implementation, C1 and C4 say no, C5 says no.
  • "Did we build a weaker neuron model and pair it with AI consciousness or does it both come from one source?" (Turn 10): under C3, brains and AI are two implementations of one kind of thing (computation); under C2, both are physical and whether either has experience is a further question (04-mind.md); under C1, both are appearances within mind.
