08 — To Nyn (τὸ νῦν): Where We Stand Right Now

The owner asked whether the matrix needs "a Greek word for now, only if there needs one." It does. Every F label in the matrix is relative to an observer position, and the position from which this whole set is written must be named and dated so later readers know what "currently" meant.

1. The word

τὸ νῦν (to nyn), "the now." Aristotle, Physics IV.10–14: the now is not a part of time but the boundary that divides past from future, the point from which before and after are measured. That is exactly what the matrix needs: a boundary marker, not a duration.

Alternatives considered and rejected:

Word Meaning Why not
καιρός (kairos) The opportune moment Implies action or opportunity; too loaded.
χρόνος (chronos) Time as duration A span, not a point.
τὸ παρόν (to paron) The present, what is at hand Acceptable, but less precise than Aristotle's boundary sense.

6. Answers to the owner's To Nyn questions

"Would quantum computing settle this once it's more advanced?" No, and the reason is structural, not a matter of maturity. A quantum computer is a physical system inside our layer obeying our layer's laws. It can settle F1 and some F2 questions inside the B1 C3 region: whether specific models of emergent spacetime, holographic codes, or lattice gauge theories are internally consistent and match data. It cannot settle whether the layer is authored (D), whether the ground is graspable (B), whether anything experiences (Axiom 6), or anything in F3/F4. Full breakdown in 09-tests.md §4.

"Do we have anything world-changing here?" Not at To Nyn. World-changing would require a prediction that (a) no existing theory makes, (b) is F1, and (c) would move an axis. The matrix contains no such prediction. What it contains is a valid partition that makes the elimination game well-posed, a corrected Fog Front that survives the owner's own objection, and a demonstration that genesis constrains morality far less than intuition says. These are useful. They are not new to philosophy.

"Has this been discovered before?" Every component, yes. See ../notes/prior-art.md and 12-named-theories-index.md.
