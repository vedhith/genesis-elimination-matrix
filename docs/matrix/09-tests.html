2. What is invalid or unsettleable, and why

  • T03 is the most important correction from the conversation. The double-slit interference pattern disappears when a which-path measurement is made by any device, conscious or not. Consciousness-causes-collapse (von Neumann–Wigner) is a fringe interpretation with no experimental support over decoherence-based accounts. No rendering shortcut is demonstrated.
  • T06, T07 fail by Axiom 6. They can test behavior, never experience.
  • T12 is underdetermined: any macro-pattern is compatible with S0, S± and D2. The problem of evil is the only established inference from world pattern to S, and it is negative (against S+), not positive.

4. Quantum computing: what it can and cannot settle

The owner's corrected question: "I meant quantum computing settle this once it's more advanced."

Capability Cell it bears on Effect

What it cannot do, at any level of maturity

Question Why not
Whether our layer is authored (D) A simulation of physics inside a simulation looks identical to a simulation of physics inside base reality. Authorship is not a computable property of the laws.
Whether the ground is graspable (B) A QC is a formal system inside our logic; it cannot step outside our logic by running faster. FG applies to it exactly as to classical machines.
Whether anything experiences (Axiom 6) A QC observes from outside like everything else.
Whether there is a terminal ground (A) Not an empirical question.
Any F3 or F4 claim By definition.

The owner's stronger hope, addressed

Gemini suggested (Turns 18–19) that quantum computers "use the same native physics that run the universe's backend" and therefore constitute a "hardware evolution designed to shift our cognitive horizon up the chart." Two problems:

  1. All computers use the universe's physics. A transistor is quantum mechanical. A QC uses superposition and entanglement as resources, which is a computational-complexity advantage, not an ontological one.
  2. Shifting p upward, in the matrix's sense, means gaining access to a higher layer or a stronger logic. A QC gives neither. It gives a faster route to answers already inside K(p_now). It moves questions from F1 to F0. It does not move anything from F3 to F1.

Net: quantum computing is a powerful instrument for the physics sub-cells of B1 C3. It is the right tool for T13, T14, T16, T17. It is not a tool for the genesis question, and no future version of it will be, for reasons that have nothing to do with qubit counts.
