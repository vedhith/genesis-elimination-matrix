15 — Does Human Morality Match the Apex?

Owner's statement: if the Unconception is the most whole thing, human morals might be good, since they would be aligned with it. Unless they are not, and what we call immorality is the aligned one. We need a system that covers every conceivable version.

The hidden bridge

"Most whole, therefore good" is a real and ancient inference, but it is a bridge premise, not a derivation. It is the convertibility thesis: being and goodness are the same thing seen two ways. Plotinus (the One is the Good), Augustine, Aquinas (ens et bonum convertuntur), Spinoza (perfection is reality). Against it: Moore's open-question argument (1903): for any property you name, "but is it good?" remains a real question, so good cannot be defined as wholeness, being, or anything else. And Gate 2: under B2b nothing, including goodness, can be read off the apex. So the inference is allowed only as a stated premise, and the matrix must carry every alternative to it.

Axis R — how human morality relates to the ground

Take H = the human moral sense, G = the value (if any) at the ground or apex. The relation is positive, negative, absent, mixed, or unstatable. Complete.

Code Relation Meaning Who holds it What would favor it
R1 Aligned H tracks G. Human good is real good. Plotinus, Aquinas, Spinoza (convertibility); natural law; Confucian tian; Stoic logos; the owner's first reading Cross-cultural convergence of moral intuitions; moral progress as discovery (Singer's expanding circle, Parfit's "climbing the same mountain")
R2 Inverted H is anti-aligned. What we call evil is what the ground favors or is. Gnosticism (the world-maker is evil, so worldly morality is his); Nietzsche's revaluation (slave morality inverts the noble); de Sade; dystheism; "nature is red in tooth and claw, and nature is the truth" Systematic anti-correlation between what flourishes in reality and what we call good; a maker shown to prefer suffering
R3 Orthogonal G has no value dimension. H is local: evolved, constructed, or both. Physicalism plus evolutionary debunking (Street, Joyce); Hume; expressivism; constructivism; Camus A complete physics with no value term; moral sense fully explained by selection with no residue
R4 Partial H tracks G through a distorting lens. Some of our good is real, some is noise. Plato's shadows; Christian "fallen but capable"; Kant's moral law within vs. inclination; Buddhist ignorance obscuring dharma; the owner's "not rooted in survival" Architect seen from below Partial convergence plus stable disagreement; moral progress that revises earlier certainties
R5 Unstatable Under B2b no relation can be asserted. H may be anything; the apex says nothing about it. Apophatic traditions; Job; the matrix's own Gate 2 Follows directly from placing the ground at B2b

What the owner's own framework implies

  • If the apex is the Unconception (B2b), the owner's position, then R5 is forced. "Most whole, therefore good" cannot be asserted. Neither can its inverse. Morality must be grounded below the apex, in nature, reason, or construction (M1b, M1c, M2), exactly as 06-morality.md §4 concluded.
  • If the apex is instead a graspable whole (B1 with C1 or C3), R1 becomes available with the convertibility premise stated, and R2 becomes available with an inversion premise stated. Neither wins by default.
  • The pop hypothesis (14-the-pop.md) adds a prediction: if each pop makes the prior level simple, then after the next pop morality should look like an effective theory of something, the way chemistry is of physics. That is compatible with R1, R3 and R4. It is incompatible with R2 only if the pop reveals harmony rather than inversion, which is exactly what cannot be known before the pop.

The owner's intuition (R1) and the owner's apex (B2b) pull apart. The matrix records both and does not resolve it, because it cannot be resolved from here.

The one thing the axis rules out

Not R2 and not R1, but the default. "Wholeness is good" and "wholeness is indifferent" are both premises. The system that covers every conceivable theory is one where neither is assumed, both are cells, and the evidence column says what would move you.
