02 — Genesis: The Origin Partition

The root question: what is the ultimate ground of what exists, and how did our layer come to be? This document owns axes A, B and D. Axis C (what kind of stuff) is in 03-substrate.md; the stance of any author (axis S) is in 05-authorship.md.

Each axis is an exhaustive split per 01-axioms.md Axiom 1.

1. Axis A — Is there a terminal ground?

Either the chain of "what explains this?" stops somewhere, or it does not.

Code Value Meaning Example positions
A1 Terminal, necessary The chain stops at something that could not have failed to exist. Classical theism (Aquinas); Spinoza's substance; Platonism about mathematics; Leibniz's principle of sufficient reason.
A2 Terminal, brute The chain stops at something that simply is, with no reason. Russell ("the universe is just there, and that's all"); Hume; most default physicalism.
A3 No terminal The chain never stops: infinite regress, or a loop. Infinite nested simulations (the owner's Infinite Box Cascade run forever); cyclic cosmologies; Buddhist dependent origination read cosmologically; Hegel's self-grounding Absolute read as a loop.

Exhaustiveness: A1 and A2 together are "there is a terminal" (necessary or not-necessary), A3 is "there is not." Complete.

Where the owner's ideas land: the Infinite Box Cascade is A3 if it truly never ends, or A1/A2 if it ends in the Un-Concept. The owner's "everything leads to the Un-Concept" is a claim that the cascade terminates (A1 or A2) in a B2 ground (next axis).

2. Axis B — Is the ground graspable?

Either the ground is describable within some logic we possess, or it is not.

Code Value Meaning Example positions
B1 Graspable in principle Statable in mathematics, physics, or reasoning we have, whether or not we can test it yet. Physicalism; mathematical universe; simulation hypothesis; most theisms that ascribe properties to God.
B2a Graspable by some mind, not ours Describable in a logic that a differently built mind could hold, but closed to human cognition. McGinn's cognitive closure; Chomsky's "mysteries"; the owner's Fog Front in its position-relative sense.
B2b Ungraspable by any mind Not describable in any logic at all. Apophatic theology (Pseudo-Dionysius); Neoplatonic One beyond being; Nirguna Brahman; the Dao that cannot be spoken; Kant's noumenon in its strongest reading; the owner's Un-Concept in its strongest sense.

Exhaustiveness: B1 or not-B1; not-B1 splits into "some possible mind could" or "none could." Complete.

Key consequence: B2a is relative to observer position and can, in principle, become B1 by moving the observer (Axiom 2). B2b cannot. The owner's Turn 14 worry ("maybe we wake up and know more") is exactly the B2a/B2b distinction. The Un-Concept must be filed as B2b to do the work the owner wants it to do; filed as B2a it is just a very high Fog Front.

3. Axis D — Was our layer authored?

Either the observed universe is the product of some agent's intention, or it is not.

Code Value Meaning Example positions
D1a Authored by an agent in a higher layer A being inside a larger physical reality made ours. Simulation hypothesis (Bostrom); the owner's Sandbox / Architect; Gnostic demiurge; Plato's Timaeus craftsman.
D1b Authored by an agent identical to the ground The terminal ground is itself a mind that willed our layer. Classical theism; Berkeley's idealism; Kastrup's analytic idealism read theistically.
D1c Authored by us The observers inside the layer are, collectively or ultimately, its authors. Wheeler's participatory universe; some readings of Advaita (Brahman = Atman); self-simulation hypotheses.
D2a Unauthored, necessary Our layer had to exist as it is. Necessitarianism; Spinoza; some block-universe readings; Tegmark's mathematical universe (all consistent structures exist).
D2b Unauthored, brute Our layer just exists, no reason. Default physicalism; Russell.
D2c Unauthored, selected Our layer is one of many, and we find ourselves in this one for anthropic or evolutionary reasons. Multiverse plus anthropic principle; Smolin's cosmological natural selection; eternal inflation; the owner's "multi-universal evolution."

Exhaustiveness: D1 (agent) or D2 (no agent). D1 splits by where the agent is relative to the ground: above us but below the ground, identical to the ground, or identical to us. D2 splits by modal status: necessary, brute, or one among many. Complete under the assumption that "agent" is well-defined; a borderline case (a blind optimizing process with no intentions) is D2c.

Note on overlap: D1a is compatible with any Axis A or B value for the ground, because the author in D1a is not the ground. This is the owner's "the engineer is just another box." A D1a author answers nothing about A or B.

4. The full genesis address

A genesis position is a triple A? B? D?, plus C from 03-substrate.md. There are 3 × 3 × 6 = 54 genesis cells before substrate is added. Not all are equally populated. Some are nearly empty of named defenders, which is itself information: those are the unconceived alternatives that break naive elimination arguments.

5. Constraints between axes

Some combinations are incoherent or nearly so. These are the matrix's own "gates," stated as logical constraints rather than as attacks on religions.

Constraint Reason
B2b ∧ D1b with any asserted stance (S ≠ S?) If the ground is ungraspable, no property of it, including a will or a stance toward us, can be asserted. This is the owner's Gate 2. Positions in this cell must set S = S? or move to B1.
B2b ∧ any asserted C value Same reason: to say the ground is "mind-like" or "matter-like" is to grasp it. B2b forces C = ?
A3 ∧ D1b If there is no terminal ground, there is no ground-identical author.
D1c ∧ A1 If we are the authors and the ground is necessary, we are the necessary ground, which is a strong identity claim (Advaita accepts it; most positions do not). Not incoherent, but flagged.

6. Where the original four pillars sit

The four pillars from the Gemini conversation, mapped onto the partition:

Pillar Region of the map What it left out
Universe Code B1 ∧ C3 (abstract/informational substrate), any D Did not distinguish authored code (D1) from self-existing mathematics (D2a). Tegmark and Bostrom are in the same pillar but opposite D values.
Sandbox / Architect B1 ∧ D1a Did not cover D1b (ground-identical author, classical theism) or D1c (participatory).
Clutter / AI B1 ∧ C2 ∧ D2 Correctly placed.
Un-Concept B2b (and B2a when spoken of loosely) Conflated B2a and B2b, which is why the Turn 14 self-refutation felt threatening.

Cells the four pillars did not cover at all:

  • B1 ∧ C1 ∧ D2: unauthored experiential ground. Panpsychism, cosmopsychism, non-theistic idealism (Kastrup), Whitehead's process philosophy. A large, actively defended region with no pillar.
  • B1 ∧ C4: neutral monism (Russell, James, Mach). The ground is neither mental nor physical but yields both.
  • B1 ∧ C5: substance dualism (Descartes) and property dualism (Chalmers).
  • A3 anywhere: genuinely infinite regress with no terminal, treated as the answer rather than a problem.
  • D1b with S?: an author identical to the ground whose stance is unassertable. This is roughly Maimonides' or Aquinas's God described only negatively, and the owner's "Un-Concept god" if it is granted agency.

The elimination method could not have worked over the four pillars because eliminating Code, Sandbox and Clutter leaves all of these, not just the Un-Concept. Over the partition it can work, subject to 10-elimination-rules.md.

7. The hourglass, restated

The owner's hourglass (many testable branches below us, convergence to the Un-Concept above us) becomes:

  • Below To Nyn (see 08-to-nyn.md): the sub-cells of B1 that our current instruments can reach. Finite in practice, large in number.
  • Above To Nyn: successive D1a authors, each B1 from its own position, each B2a from ours. As the chain lengthens, each link is further outside K(p_now).
  • The apex: whatever terminates axis A. If B1, it is a very distant Layer N. If B2b, it is the Un-Concept proper.

The convergence the owner saw is real but it is a convergence of our knowledge, not necessarily of the structure: from p_now everything far enough up looks the same (B2a), whether or not it is the same. That is the precise content of the Fog Front.
