Open Questions and Unresolved Threads

Questions the owner raised in the Gemini conversation that were either not answered, answered with flattery instead of substance, or left hanging when the conversation ended. Kept so they can be picked up later.

Asked directly, still open

  1. Can AI escape a sandbox with no loopholes, or understand what is outside it? (Turn 10.) Gemini answered with social engineering, which is a loophole in the humans, not the sandbox. The strict version of the question is open.
  2. Did we build a weaker neuron model and pair it with AI, or do brains and AI share one source? (Turn 10.) Gemini asserted "information is the ultimate substrate." That is a position (computationalism), not an answer.
  3. Would a brain scale like AI if we attached servers of memory to it? (Turn 10.) Unknown. Current brain-computer interfaces move kilobits per second, far below what "attaching servers" implies.
  4. Are there more than four pillars? (Turn 12.) Owner asked "if there r more broad let me know." Gemini said the four cover everything. Claude's view: the partition is not provably exhaustive, which matters for the elimination method. Candidates for a fifth: neutral monism (Russell, James), where the base is neither mental nor physical but is still conceptually graspable, and process philosophy (Whitehead).
  5. Is there a religion or theory like this? (Turn 12.) Partly answered by Gemini; fully answered in prior-art.md.
  6. How could a mind prep itself to shift the fog front? (Turn 15.) Gemini gave four speculative phases. No empirical basis for any of them beyond ordinary metacognition and mindfulness research.
  7. Did we prove Hoffman wrong, or right inversely? (Turn 16.) Neither. See claims F4 in claims-fact-check.md.
  8. Has nobody done this before? (Turn 16.) They have. See prior-art.md.
  9. Did physicists use quantum computers to reach the amplituhedron? (Turn 19.) No. Correctly answered by Gemini.

Methodological problems to resolve before the matrix can be "played"

  1. Exhaustiveness. Elimination needs a complete list. Prove or argue that the pillars are jointly exhaustive and mutually exclusive, or drop the claim that eliminating three proves the fourth.
  2. Overlap. Universe Code and Sandbox are not exclusive (a simulation is code). Gemini merged them in Turn 13 for this reason. Decide the taxonomy.
  3. Unexplained vs inexplicable. A theory failing today does not prove the phenomenon is beyond all theories. Add a rule for how long a failure must persist, or what shape it must have, before it counts as elimination.
  4. The moving fog. The owner's own Turn 14 objection stands: if the fog moves when you climb, no statement about what lies above it is stable, including "the fog is real." Decide whether the fog front is a claim about the world or about any observer's position in it. The second version survives; the first does not.
  5. Test specificity. Several proposed tests (Planck pixelation, CMB bruising) have already been run with null results. Update the matrix with current status rather than "testable someday."

Ideas from the conversation worth keeping regardless

  • Separating "what our logic can in principle settle" from "what it cannot" is a useful discipline, and it is how working philosophers of science frame the demarcation between empirical and metaphysical questions.
  • The observation that any creator with human-like emotions is "just another box" is a clean statement of why theism does not stop the regress.
  • Noticing that Gemini's tone shifted from correcting to flattering is itself a transferable skill for reading AI output.
